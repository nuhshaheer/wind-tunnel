Findings from drawing it out

Gaps that showed up when the parts were first placed together (2026-11-09), and how the sizing of 2026-11-26 settled them.

  1. Settling-chamber length grows with the chosen parts. Resolved: compact spacing, 275 in all (D-027). Full-decay spacing would have been about 465.
  2. Taps “one per wall” meet the lift-off panels. Resolved: E sits in a printed entrance band closed on all four sides; M uses one tap in each side wall, 12 above the floor; no tap is in the lift-off panel (D-024).
  3. The M-station floor tap cannot sit at the floor centre. Resolved: the floor tap is dropped; M has two side-wall taps (D-024).
  4. 250 mm sections need splitting in cross-section. Resolved: each prints as four identical corner pieces glued at mid-wall seams (D-023); the contraction splits at x = 82, so its exit is one seamless piece.
  5. The balance box sets the tunnel’s height. Resolved: with the mount stacked on the cell the box reaches 145 below the axis, the chamber’s flanges 140; the axis stands 175 above the bench, so the box clears by 30 (cad/sizing/supports.md).
  6. I²C length limits where the Arduino box can go. Placed: on the bench beside the contraction, with the SDP810 on the black wall near the test-section entrance and the BME280 on the fan stand (Proposed).
  7. Turning the sting-lock knob twists the load cell. Still a caution: the screw stays along the wind, so any twist that gets through bends the cell in its stiff direction; hold the mount while turning (D-015).
